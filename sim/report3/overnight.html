1. Blender is now the animation tool

The duck rig lives in anim/duck.blend: one bone per servo with the hinge locked to the bone's Z axis, so the joint angle you key is the angle the servo gets. Foot IK with a slider, left/right mirroring with the duck's real sign table, and a sidebar panel with import, export, check, mirror and loop. Round-tripping a recorded turn clip through Blender reproduces it to the last digit. How to animate is in anim/README.md; open it with anim/blender.sh.

Three real defects were found and fixed on the way: MuJoCo recentres every mesh at compile time (raw STL vertices never matched the compiled positions), the IK driver did not refresh when the slider changed from Python, and the MJCF home keyframe sits 17 mm too low (feet through the floor). The rig now uses the settled standing height.

3. Motion graph runtime: skills hand off without stopping

graph_runtime/ is the state machine from the framework: explicit edges, entry windows on gait phase and contact, blending at the reference level (the commanded joint targets cross-fade over the window, two policies are never averaged), previous actions handed to the incoming policy, timeouts, and abort into a sit skill. It runs on MuJoCo now and has a locked hardware backend that documents the exact runtime calls for later.

Finding: the placeholder bow played open loop with the legs parked at the static home pose tips the duck forward 1.1 s in (5 of 5). The head carries a lot of the mass. That is exactly what a tracking policy must learn to compensate, and a reason for the clip gate to grow a head-excursion check.

4. Skill packages, battery, sim-to-sim, regression

Every skill is a folder skills/<name>/v<N>/ with skill.json, the clip, the ONNX and reports; a loader refuses anything incomplete. The current walk brain is packaged as walk/v1 and put through the strict battery: 5 seeds × 10 episodes per test, randomised friction, mass and torque, identical fall thresholds.

Sim-to-sim runs the package through the real runtime code with a mocked servo bus: 0.0200 rad RMS target difference, a hair over the 0.02 bar, and the cause is isolated. Finding: the current walk brain does not step at all with the 8 Hz action filter (0.001 m/s). The filter belongs to the newer curriculum brains. If the duck ever stood and refused to walk, this is a candidate.

5. Mechatronics: the envelope, with sources

mechatronics/envelope.json gives every deploy limit a status (verified, measured, derived, proposed, unverified) and a source file. Torque 1.86 N·m, 4 rad/s, fall tilt 45°, thermal warn 50 / cool-down 60 / hard 65 °C proposed, voltage guards, per-joint deploy ranges.

  • The "8 Hz" action filter is a 1.2 Hz filter. The formula puts 1/fc where an RC constant belongs. Sim and runtime share it, so nothing is inconsistent, but packages should carry the real cutoff.
  • Mass is the largest known model error: the MJCF totals 2.107 kg against your 1.5 to 1.8 kg estimate. mechatronics/weigh_me.md is a 10-minute kitchen-scale checklist.
  • 8.3 V flag: almost certainly the servos' 8.0 V max-voltage register tripping after a full charge; advisory by default. The read-only health script confirms it once the Pi is on (it was off tonight).
  • At the 1.86 N·m cap the model's steady-speed ceiling is 3.2 rad/s, below the 3.8 to 4.5 rad/s the real knees reach: the cap is right at stall and pessimistic at speed.

7. mjlab spike

The duck's MJCF loads into mjlab unchanged, BAM servo model and all; a duck tracking environment builds and trains PPO at about 33k env-steps/s at 1024 environments on the 4060, with a converter prototype from our clip JSON. Recommendation: stay on brax/MJX for phase 1, port episodic tracking to mjlab in phase 2, about 3 to 4 days to a first clip there. Details in experiments/mjlab_spike.md.

8. The first skills trained from clips

The Controls team built the tracking task in train_pg: the BeyondMimic-style reward template (body position, orientation and velocities, joint tracking, action rate, joint limits, self-contact), adaptive start sampling, a fixed randomisation set, the previous two actions in the observation, and the deploy filters inside training. Every number lives in recipe_v1.py with the reason it differs from the humanoid paper. The policy outputs a correction on top of the clip, so a zero action plays the clip as authored.
