Hands on

Every servo has a sensor that knows its angle, and a motor that can hold or move it. With the motor off (grey needle) you can turn the horn by hand and watch the dial follow. Drag a dial, and that servo's motor switches on (blue needle) and moves to the orange line.

Try it: switch a motor on and push against the horn. The orange effort bar and the ⚡ current go up, because the motor works harder to hold its place.

Follow the leader

Pick a leader with its Lead button. The leader's motor turns off, so you can twist it by hand. The Pi reads its angle about 50 times a second and tells all the other servos to copy it. That's how a puppet controller or a robot arm "teach" mode works.

No leader yet: press Lead on any servo.

Wave

Each servo follows the same swing, but a little later than the one before it, so a wave rolls along the line. Robots walk with the same trick: legs repeat a pattern, each one shifted in time.

Record & replay

Press Record and every motor goes limp. Move the horns by hand and the Pi saves all 14 angles many times a second. Press Play and the motors repeat your moves exactly. Animators use the same idea to teach robots new moves.
